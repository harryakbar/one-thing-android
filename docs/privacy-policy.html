Privacy Policy

One Thing — Last updated: June 2025

Overview

One Thing is a minimalist daily focus app. We take privacy seriously: the app collects no personal data and does not communicate with any external servers.

Data We Collect

We collect nothing. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No advertising identifiers are accessed.
  • No data is transmitted off your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your daily task entries (text and completion status) in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

One Thing does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
